## Summary
- Best classical: Frozen MobileNetV3 + Logistic Regression, 96.4%.
- Best quantum (pixel features): QKC ZZ-full, 8 qubits, 64.0%. This is in line with the classical RBF-SVM on the same PCA-8 pixels (64.8%).
- Best hybrid: MobileNetV3 → PCA 8 + QKC product-RY, 93.2%. The best classical classifier on the *same* 8 features scores 93.0%: a +0.3 pp gap, inside ±2.2 pp (two seed sds).
- **The hybrid gain comes from the classical CNN features** (+29 to +36 pp over pixel-PCA quantum kernels), not from the quantum classifier.
  The classifier effect is within seed noise at k = 4, 6 and 8. **No quantum advantage is claimed.**
- Product-RY is an unentangled feature map whose kernel has a closed classical form. It is among the best hybrids, which supports the conclusion above.
